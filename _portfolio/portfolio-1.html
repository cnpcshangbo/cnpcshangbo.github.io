WHY SYNCHRONIZATION IS THE HARD PART

Sub-Frame Temporal & Spatial Alignment at Highway Speeds

At 65 mph (29 m/s), a 50 ms clock offset between a spinning 3D LiDAR and an RGB camera shifts projected bounding boxes by 1.45 meters — smearing multi-frame point-cloud reconstruction and collapsing downstream VRU/freight classification.

STAGE 01

Multi-Sensor Ingestion

Continuous capture of raw 3D LiDAR UDP sweeps and high-resolution RGB streams across 743 unattended field sessions.

STAGE 02

Temporal Drift Correction

Timestamp alignment and online clock-skew compensation between LiDAR hardware timestamps and camera exposure triggers.

STAGE 03

6-DoF Extrinsic Calibration

Rigid spatial calibration mapping 3D point clouds onto RGB image planes across varying mast heights and tilt geometries.

STAGE 04

Background Modeling

Exploiting stationary roadside geometry to strip guardrails, overpasses, and foliage before 3D proposal generation.

STAGE 05

CNN 3D VRU & Freight Detection

Config-portable 3D detection classifying passenger cars, multi-unit freight trucks, motorcycles, cyclists, and pedestrians.

STAGE 06

Multi-Frame Vehicle Stitching

Rigid-body registration accumulating sparse distant LiDAR returns across consecutive sweeps into dense per-vehicle 3D models.

NYSDOT I-95 BENCHMARK · INFRALIDAR-EVAL

90.1% Fixed-Sensor Crossing Recovery vs. 72.9% Ego Baseline

Off-the-shelf 3D detectors trained on ego-vehicle datasets (KITTI, Waymo, nuScenes) assume a sensor moving at bumper/roof height (~1.8 m). Mounted on an elevated roadside mast overlooking Interstate 95 in the Bronx, those ego-centric models drop to 72.9% vehicle crossing recovery due to pitch/height domain shift and far-lane sparsity.

  • Fixed-Geometry Conditioning (90.1%): Incorporating stationary background priors, ground-plane rectification, and temporal track continuity recovers +17.2 percentage points on live highway traffic.
  • 61.9-Hour Failure Taxonomy: Documents sensor thermal drift, rain/spray backscatter, heavy-truck shadow handoffs, and beam-count sensitivity across 743 sessions.

From Dense 3D Truck Profiles to Citywide Curbside Freight Sensing
