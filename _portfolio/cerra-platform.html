CERRA: Auditable Geospatial AI & Traffic Simulation for Public Agencies

Public transportation agencies cannot make signal-timing, work-zone, or freight-corridor decisions from opaque black-box LLM chat replies. Built and operated at the University of Maryland, CERRA pairs real corridor telemetry with a protected LLM-SUMO microscopic simulation bridge — returning observed metrics, simulated counterfactuals, explicit assumptions, and verifiable data sources side by side.

NO HALLUCINATED TRAFFIC ENGINEERING

The Four-Part Auditable Decision Output

CERRA is an AI-driven geospatial research platform built for public agencies at UMD M-TRAIL (under Prof. Xianfeng Yang). Rather than asking a language model to guess queue lengths, CERRA translates natural-language corridor questions into deterministic data queries and sandboxed SUMO simulation runs.
