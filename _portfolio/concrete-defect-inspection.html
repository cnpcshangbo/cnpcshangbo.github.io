MICRO SCALE · IEEE T-ASE 2025 · SELECTED FOR IEEE IROS 2025

Robotic Concrete Defect Localization on 3D Structural Meshes

Raw inspection video gives civil engineers thousands of disconnected 2D crack snapshots with no global coordinates or metric scale. Developed at the CCNY Robotics Lab, this end-to-end system connects a wall-climbing robot and iPad operator app to AWS serverless CNN segmentation and WebODM 3D point-cloud registration — localizing and measuring cracks directly on full-scale concrete columns and seawalls.

FIELD-TO-CLOUD ENGINEERING

iPad Operator App & AWS Serverless Defect Engine

To make robotic defect analytics usable by field inspectors rather than only lab researchers, I built and deployed the cloud-connected mobile workflow:

  • iOS/iPadOS Control & MapView App: Operates the climbing robot via TestFlight, logs RGB + depth + gravity vectors per frame, and pins inspection sessions onto an interactive asset map.
  • 4-Stage AWS Lambda Pipeline: Automatically ingests raw session imagery into Amplify/S3, runs deep CNN crack segmentation, generates visual overlays, and computes dimension metrics.

3D LOCALIZATION & MEDIAL-AXIS SKELETONIZATION

Turning Pixel Masks into Engineering-Grade Crack Topology

Binary segmentation masks alone cannot tell a structural engineer whether a hairline fracture grew by 4 cm since the last biennial cycle. Our analytics pipeline bridges pixels to structural mechanics:

  • 2D-to-3D Texture & Point-Cloud Fusion: Registers segmented defect masks directly onto a WebODM 3D structure-from-motion model of the entire concrete pier or seawall.
  • Morphological Skeletonization: Extracts 1-pixel medial-axis crack skeletons with homography/depth scale calibration to compute metric crack length and orientation.
  • Subsurface NDT Companion Work: Complemented by automated acoustic impact-echo and rotary-percussive sounding analytics plus thermal IR delamination detection.
