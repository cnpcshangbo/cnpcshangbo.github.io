PHYSICS-GROUNDED METRIC · PACO

Why Expanding Ego Field-of-View Cannot Solve Occlusion

Standard autonomous-driving benchmarks weight every object equally regardless of collision risk or line-of-sight physics. PACO (Proactive Risk-Weighted Cooperative Observability) couples 3D ray-cast occlusion geometry with kinematic conflict risk on real NYC urban LiDAR data:

  • 120° ADAS (0.37) → 360° AV (0.61) → Roadside V2I (1.00): Even an ideal 360° vehicle sensor loses 39% of risk-weighted intersection observability behind buses, delivery vans, and turning queues.
  • Freight Multiplies Blind Zones: Heavy urban freight nearly doubles occluded road users per frame from 1.7 to 3.3.
  • Placement Guidance: Turns qualitative V2I arguments into quantitative mast-placement criteria for municipal DOTs.

PROACTIVE SAFETY AUDITING · CVPRW DRIVEX 2026

Diagnosing 180 Near-Miss Conflicts Before a Crash Occurs

Traditional traffic safety waits years for fatal or injurious crashes to accumulate. Using continuous 3D LiDAR trajectories at a complex NYC intersection, our proactive safety auditor extracts sub-meter Post-Encroachment Time (PET) and Time-to-Collision (TTC) conflict pairs automatically:

  • Spatial Conflict Heatmaps (n = 180): Pinpoints exact crosswalk conflict pockets where permissive turning vehicles encroach on pedestrians.
  • Micro-Behavioral Kinematics: Captures vehicle deceleration failures, abrupt pedestrian hesitation, and occluded curb-entry conflicts invisible to monocular traffic cameras.
  • Actionable Signal Countermeasures: Directly supports Leading Pedestrian Intervals (LPI), protected turn phases, and curb-daylighting redesigns.
