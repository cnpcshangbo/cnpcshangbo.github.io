MICRO SCALE · U.S. PATENT 12,296,994 B2 · USDOT INSPIRE

BIRDS: Hybrid Flying & Girder-Clamping Bridge Robot

Conventional inspection drones drain batteries in minutes while fighting under-bridge wind gusts without GPS. BIRDS flies up to a steel I-girder, locks onto the bottom flange via onboard NVIDIA Jetson RGB-D vision, clamps in 1.3 seconds, shuts off its rotors, and rolls smoothly along the bridge span.

PATENTED DUAL-SUBSYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

Decoupling Flight Endurance from Inspection Duration

Co-invented under U.S. Patent 12,296,994 B2, BIRDS integrates two tightly coordinated ROS subsystems with human-in-the-loop supervision via a custom iOS operator app:

  • Flying & Multi-Camera UAS Payload: Synchronized visible, thermal infrared, and hyperspectral imaging with onboard time-tagging, Vicon/optical-flow indoor validation, and GPS-denied hover stabilization.
  • Clamping & Traversing Mechanism: Dual V-arm linear actuators with compliant flange pads and motorized rollers that convert the aircraft into a rail-guided inspection cart on steel I-girders.
  • Companion Scour Robot: Paired with an underwater acoustic imaging robot (ROS + Arduino + embedded Linux) for submerged bridge foundation scour assessment.

ASYMMETRIC ACTUATION & 3D TRAJECTORY LOGS

1.3-Second Urgent Capture & Verified 3D Perching Stability

When hovering centimeters beneath a bridge girder in turbulent crosswinds, slow mechanical grippers risk blade strikes or missed perches. We engineered an asymmetric PID/fractional-order actuator profile:

  • 1.3-Second Rapid Closure: High-speed inward stroke locks onto the I-girder flange immediately upon RGB-D standoff trigger while regulating grip force.
  • 8.5-Second Controlled Release: Smooth, damped opening stroke ensures predictable weight transfer back to the flight controller prior to departure.
  • 3D Motion-Capture Validation: Logged North-East-Down trajectories verify smooth vertical ascent, bounded lateral drift during clamping contact, and steady post-clamp perch.
