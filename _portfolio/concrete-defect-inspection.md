## What it is

A separate project from BIRDS, not a BIRDS outcome. This work localizes and visualizes structural defects of concrete infrastructure from robotic inspection and data analytics.

The author lists make the separation clear: apart from Bo Shang, the two projects share no authors. BIRDS is the Missouri S&T / INSPIRE bridge-inspection robot deployment project (Chen group); this paper is Feng, Shang, Hoxha, Hernández, He, Wang, and Xiao.

## Publication

- Jinglun Feng, Bo Shang, Ejup Hoxha, César Hernández, Yang He, Weihan Wang, Jizhong Xiao. **Robotic Inspection and Data Analytics to Localize and Visualize the Structural Defects of Concrete Infrastructure.** *IEEE Transactions on Automation Science and Engineering, vol. 22, pp. 22324–22336*, 2025. Journal paper selected for presentation at IEEE IROS 2025. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/TASE.2025.3535227)
