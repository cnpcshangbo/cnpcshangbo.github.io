MICRO SCALE · NONLINEAR DYNAMICS 2019 · ICUAS 2016 / 2017

Iso-Damping Fractional-Order UAV Visual Servoing

Under bridges and inside GPS-denied caves, small inspection drones rely on onboard vision whose frame rate drops and jitters under compute load. By replacing integer-order PID with an iso-damping fractional-order PD^μ flight controller, we expanded the maximum stable vision sampling period by 71% (0.546 s → 0.933 s) and improved outdoor hovering precision by 20%.

FREQUENCY & FLIGHT-DOMAIN VALIDATION

Iso-Damping Step Response & 20% Tighter Outdoor Hovering

By enforcing zero phase derivative at the gain crossover frequency, the fractional differentiation order μ flattens the Bode phase curve — keeping step-response overshoot invariant when battery voltage drops or payload mass changes, and tightening real flight pixel error dispersion by 20%.
