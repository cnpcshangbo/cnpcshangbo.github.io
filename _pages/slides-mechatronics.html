RESEARCH THESIS · WHY FIELD MECHATRONICS IS HARD 03 / 26

Co-Designing Mechanisms, Embedded Sensing, and Robust Control

1. Reconfigurable Mechanisms

  • Pure multirotors exhaust batteries in minutes while fighting under-bridge wind gusts.
  • Mechatronic solution: Dual-mode aerial flight + rapid 1.3 s mechanical flange clamping + motorized surface rolling (US Patent 12,296,994 B2), plus wall-climbing NDT robots.

2. Embedded Multi-Sensor Fusion

  • GPS and magnetometers fail beneath steel bridge decks; asynchronous sensors smear 3D geometry.
  • Mechatronic solution: Onboard RGB-D flange/heading perception on Jetson/RPi, quadrature encoder limit loops, and sub-frame LiDAR-camera clock synchronization.

3. Delay- & Gain-Robust Control

  • Onboard vision latency, payload shifts, and wind disturbances destabilize integer-order PID loops.
  • Mechatronic solution: Iso-damping fractional-order PD^μ control expanding maximum vision sampling period by +71% (0.546 s → 0.933 s) with flat crossover phase.

Synthesis: Core Principle: Treat mechanism compliance, sensor latency, and feedback control as a single coupled co-design problem rather than isolated layers.

THRUST II · PROBLEM FORMULATION & CONTROL STRUCTURE 14 / 26

Quadrotor Visual Servoing Under Wind & Vision Latency

Why Classical Integer-Order PID Struggles on Small UAVs

  • GPS-Denied Drift: Under bridges, indoors, and inside caves, position hold relies on onboard monocular/RGB-D visual servoing.
  • Coupled Disturbances: Aerodynamic gusts, battery voltage sag (changing actuator gain K), and low-FPS embedded vision delay erode phase margin.
  • Cascaded Outer/Inner Loop: Outer visual position controller C(s) commands the inner autopilot velocity loop V(s) = k / (τs + 1) cascaded with kinematic integrator 1/s.
Cascaded visual-servoing feedback loop: C(s) outer vision controller driving speed loop V(s) and 1/s integrator.

THRUST II · FREQUENCY-DOMAIN ISO-DAMPING DESIGN 15 / 26

Extending Integer PID to Iso-Damping Fractional-Order PD^μ

Three Exact Frequency-Domain Specifications at ω_c

  • Controller Law: C(s) = K_p + K_d s^μ (continuous fractional differentiation order μ ∈ (0, 2) adds an analytical degree of freedom).
  • 1. Phase Margin: Arg[C(jω_c) P(jω_c)] = −π + φ_m.
  • 2. Iso-Damping (Flat Phase): | d Arg[C(jω) P(jω)] / dω |_{ω=ω_c} = 0.
  • 3. Unity Gain Crossover: |C(jω_c) P(jω_c)|_{dB} = 0.
  • Physical Meaning: Zero phase slope around ω_c preserves phase margin even when plant gain or delay shifts!
Open-loop Bode comparison: FOPD achieves a flat phase plateau across crossover ω_c unlike steep IOPID phase roll-off.

FUTURE VISION · 5-YEAR MECHATRONICS LAB AGENDA 24 / 26

Research Statement: Next-Generation Field Mechatronics Lab

Pillar 1 · Reconfigurable & Contact-Rich Field Robots

  • Next-gen aerial-perching, magnetic/vortex climbing, and multi-modal rolling mechanisms.
  • Compliant force/impedance end-effectors for contact acoustic, ultrasonic, and eddy-current NDT.
  • On-the-fly adaptive / liquid neural policies running on low-power edge NPUs.

Pillar 2 · Delay- & Uncertainty-Certified Control

  • Fractional-order and physics-informed nonlinear control with provable delay & gain margins.
  • Safe handoff between foundation-model high-level planners and deterministic real-time MCU/RTOS servo loops.
  • Wind-gust & contact-transition disturbance rejection.

Pillar 3 · Synchronized Sensor Pods & HIL Twins

  • Open-hardware sub-millisecond synchronized 3D LiDAR + RGB + thermal + acoustic mechatronic pods.
  • Hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) validation benches coupling physical actuators/sensors with calibrated digital twins.
  • Turnkey field partnerships with DOTs, utilities & manufacturing.

Synthesis: Lab Mission: Advance the science of coupled mechanism-sensor-control co-design so autonomous robots operate safely, enduringly, and auditably in harsh real-world environments.
